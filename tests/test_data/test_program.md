### 1. Introduction and Program Context
Here's a full body workout program focused on maximizing muscle hypertrophy and strength. This program is designed to be performed three times a week with rest days in between, ensuring recovery and optimal muscle growth.

### 2. Volume (Sets per Muscle Group)
- **Chest**: 9 sets per week
- **Back**: 9 sets per week
- **Biceps**: 6 sets per week
- **Triceps**: 6 sets per week
- **Quads**: 9 sets per week
- **Hamstrings**: 9 sets per week
- **Shoulders**: 6 sets per week
- **Calves**: 6 sets per week

### 3. Workout Program

#### Day 1
- **Warm-up**: Light jog or dynamic stretching

- **Squats**: 3 sets of 6-8 reps
  - Intensity: 2-3 RIR
  - Rest: 3 minutes
  - Progression: Double progression model

- **Bench Press**: 3 sets of 6-8 reps
  - Intensity: 1-2 RIR
  - Rest: 2.5 minutes
  - Progression: Double progression model

- **Barbell Row**: 3 sets of 8-10 reps
  - Intensity: 1-2 RIR
  - Rest: 2.5 minutes
  - Progression: Dynamic Double Progression

- **Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press**: 3 sets of 8-10 reps
  - Intensity: 1-2 RIR
  - Rest: 2 minutes
  - Progression: Double progression model

- **Standing Calf Raises**: 3 sets of 12-15 reps
  - Intensity: 0-1 RIR
  - Rest: 1 minute
  - Progression: Dynamic Double Progression

#### Rest Day

#### Day 2
- **Warm-up**: Light jog or dynamic stretching

- **Deadlifts**: 2 sets of 5-7 reps
  - Intensity: 2-3 RIR
  - Rest: 3.5 minutes
  - Progression: Double progression model

- **Incline Dumbbell Press**: 3 sets of 8-10 reps
  - Intensity: 1-2 RIR
  - Rest: 2 minutes
  - Progression: Dynamic Double Progression

- **Pull-ups**: 3 sets of 6-8 reps
  - Intensity: 1-2 RIR
  - Rest: 2 minutes
  - Progression: Double progression model

- **Tricep Pushdowns**: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  - Intensity: 0-1 RIR
  - Rest: 1.5 minutes
  - Progression: Dynamic Double Progression

- **Bicep Curls**: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  - Intensity: 0-1 RIR
  - Rest: 1.5 minutes
  - Progression: Double progression model

#### Rest Day

#### Day 3
- **Warm-up**: Light jog or dynamic stretching

- **Leg Press**: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  - Intensity: 1-2 RIR
  - Rest: 2.5 minutes
  - Progression: Dynamic Double Progression

- **Lateral Raises**: 2 sets of 12-15 reps
  - Intensity: 0-1 RIR
  - Rest: 1.5 minutes
  - Progression: Double progression model

- **Bent Over Flyes**: 2 sets of 12-15 reps
  - Intensity: 0-1 RIR
  - Rest: 1.5 minutes
  - Progression: Double progression model

- **Leg Curls**: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  - Intensity: 1-2 RIR
  - Rest: 2 minutes
  - Progression: Dynamic Double Progression

- **Standing Calf Raise**: 3 sets of 12-15 reps
  - Intensity: 0-1 RIR
  - Rest: 1 minute
  - Progression: Double progression model

This program ensures balanced growth and sufficient recovery time. Enjoy your training!
